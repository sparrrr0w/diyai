DIY.ai Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 28, 2026

DIY.ai ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how. We've designed DIY.ai to keep everything on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

DIY.ai does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data with us or any third party. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email, phone number, or contact information
  • Your location
  • Your photos, audio, or text input
  • Repair guides, history, or favorites you create in the app
  • Device identifiers used for tracking or advertising
  • Crash logs, analytics, or usage telemetry

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following stays only on your device and is never transmitted to us:

  • Repair guides you generate
  • Photos you take or import for repair lookup
  • Voice recordings (if any) used for speech recognition
  • Favorites and history of generated guides
  • AI-generated step illustrations
  • Your sign-in identifier from Sign in with Apple (if used)

This data is deleted when you delete the app.

3. On-Device AI Processing

DIY.ai uses Apple's on-device generative AI frameworks — specifically the Foundation Models framework and Image Playground — to generate repair guides and step illustrations. All AI processing happens on your device. Your prompts, photos, and voice input are never sent to our servers because we do not operate any servers.

4. Apple Services Used by the App

The app integrates with the following Apple services, which are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy:

  • Sign in with Apple (optional): If you choose to sign in, Apple provides us with a unique user identifier. We store this identifier only on your device.
  • Apple In-App Purchases (StoreKit): For optional Pro subscriptions. Apple processes the transaction; we do not see your payment information.
  • Apple Intelligence (Foundation Models, Image Playground): For on-device guide and illustration generation.
  • Speech Recognition: Apple's on-device speech recognition converts your spoken input to text. Audio is not stored or transmitted.

5. Camera, Microphone, Speech, and Photo Permissions

The app may request access to:

  • Camera — so you can take photos of items you want to repair.
  • Microphone — so you can describe problems out loud.
  • Speech Recognition — to convert your spoken words to text.
  • Photo Library — to add existing photos to repair guides.

All of these are optional. You can deny or revoke permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app. The data captured under these permissions stays on your device.

6. Children's Privacy

DIY.ai is suitable for all ages (rated 4+). We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the Effective Date above and post the revised policy at this URL.
