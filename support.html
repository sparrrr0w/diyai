Frequently Asked Questions

The app says "Apple Intelligence isn't available." What do I do?

DIY.ai relies on Apple Intelligence for on-device guide generation. Open the Settings app on your device, tap Apple Intelligence & Siri, and turn Apple Intelligence on. If the option doesn't appear, your device isn't supported (see System Requirements above).

How do I get the AI-generated illustrations on each step?

Illustrations are part of the DIY.ai Pro subscription. Open Settings inside the app and tap Upgrade. Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID, not the app:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Tap DIY.ai Pro and choose Cancel Subscription
How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

Is my data shared or uploaded anywhere?

No. DIY.ai uses Apple's on-device AI. Your photos, prompts, voice input, and saved guides stay on your device. We do not operate any servers or collect any data. See our Privacy Policy.

I'm seeing a permission error for camera / microphone / photos.

Open the iOS Settings app, scroll down to DIY.ai, and toggle the permissions on. You can also tap "Reset Location & Privacy" in Settings → General → Transfer or Reset iPhone if needed.

The AI-generated guide is wrong or unsafe. What should I do?

AI-generated content can be inaccurate. Please don't follow any guide that looks unsafe — when in doubt, consult a licensed professional. See our Terms of Service for important safety disclaimers.
